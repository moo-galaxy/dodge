Understanding Turbochargers

How They Work and Why They Matter

What is a Turbocharger?

A turbocharger (or turbo) is a forced induction device that enhances engine performance by increasing air intake, leading to better fuel combustion and power output.

How Does a Turbocharger Work?

A turbocharger consists of two main components:

  • Turbine Side (Hot Side): Exhaust gases spin the turbine, which powers the compressor.
  • Compressor Side (Cold Side): The compressor pulls in and compresses air before sending it into the engine.

Benefits of Turbocharging

Increased Power

Boosts engine power without increasing size.

Fuel Efficiency

Uses exhaust gases to optimize fuel usage.

Lower Emissions

Reduces fuel consumption and emissions.
